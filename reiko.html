Personality & Background

II / Character

The confident and prideful ruler of the Pride Kingdom, often coming across as arrogant or lacking humility. However, beneath her prideful exterior, she has a loving and caring heart, especially toward her people, whom she deeply cherishes and protects. She also holds a special love for Faron, who is one of her greatest prides and someone she deeply treasures.


Abilities & Traits

III / Capabilities

Primary Ability

Her ability allows her to sense someone's whereabouts simply by knowing their true name. Through this connection, she can also perceive their current emotions, feelings, location, and the sin they are experiencing or embracing at that moment.

Secondary Ability

Reiko can create powerful runes capable of restraining and holding back almost anything, making them highly effective for containment and imprisonment.

Strengths

Reiko is highly intelligent, strategic, and quick-witted, allowing her to make sound decisions even in difficult situations. She is also a skilled fighter with strong combat abilities, making her capable both on and off the battlefield. Her mastery of runes and unique sensing abilities further enhance her versatility, making her a formidable ruler and opponent.
