PERSONALITY & BACKGROUND

02 / CHARACTER

Samael Duropo is a rude, cruel, and hateful individual who rarely shows any regard for others. As Cleptosacra's all-rounder, he is responsible for carrying out whatever his father demands of him, seemingly willing to do anything to fulfill his father's wishes. However, beneath his obedience lies an ambition of his own. He wants nothing more than to take everything Eurelin has, driven by resentment and an insatiable desire for everything that belongs to them. He is not someone who easily forgives or forgets, and his hatred towards those he considers his enemies knows little restraint.
