        <h2>The Beginning of an Era</h2>
    </section>

    <section class="overview">
        <p>
            The First War was a continental conflict between
            Pride and the other kingdoms of Aedyr. What began
            as growing political tension eventually developed
            into a war that would determine the future of
            the Seven Kingdoms.
        </p>

        <p>
            Following Reiko's rise to power and Pride's
            independence from Greed, the balance of power
            across the continent began to change. Pride
            gradually grew stronger, while relations between
            the kingdoms became increasingly strained.
        </p>

        <p>
            On July 22, 1677, Reiko officially declared war
            against the other kingdoms. Greed, Wrath, Lust,
            Envy, and eventually Gluttony formed a coalition
            against Pride. Despite their common enemy,
            disagreements and conflicting interests weakened
            the alliance from within.
        </p>

        <p>
            As the years passed, the coalition gradually
            fell apart. Kingdoms began to fall, alliances
            shifted, and Pride continued its campaigns
            across the continent. On December 12, 1683,
            the First War officially came to an end.
        </p>
    </section>
