BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE WORLD

Aedyr was once a land of flawless balance, created as a perfect world free from corruption. Entrusted with its care was a witch, a creation of God whose purpose was to govern the world, watch over its inhabitants, and maintain the harmony of all things. She was also the source of humanity's extraordinary abilities, granting gifts and powers to those who inhabited the world. Despite her divine purpose, the witch possessed a curious soul, always longing to discover what lay beyond the boundaries of Aedyr.

Her curiosity eventually led her to Earth, where she encountered a world unlike her own. There, she fell in love with a mortal and bore a child, allowing herself to become attached to a life far from the one she had been entrusted to oversee. Her prolonged absence left Aedyr without its guardian, disrupting the balance she had once maintained. When God discovered what had become of his creation, his anger was immeasurable. Viewing her actions as a betrayal of her divine purpose, he condemned her to a terrible fate. The witch was stripped of her existence, and her soul was torn apart into seven fragments, each embodying a different sin.

From these fragments emerged the Seven Sins, born from the very soul that had once protected the world. They became the foundation of its corruption.

Ever since, sin has become an inseparable part of life in Aedyr. Pride, greed, envy, lust, gluttony, wrath, and sloth have taken root within humanity, their influence reaching every corner of the land. Yet the witch's gifts did not disappear entirely with her. The people of Aedyr continue to possess extraordinary abilities, though their powers and the nature of their souls became linked with the nation they're in. Only a select few are born untouched by the corruption of sin, their souls remaining pure and untainted. They are rare, almost like remnants of the world as it once was. The majority, however, are born carrying the stain of sin, some embracing it willingly, while others fall victim to its influence without ever understanding its origin.

And so, Aedyr remains a world divided between the few who are born pure and the many who live beneath the rule of the Seven Sins, the shattered remnants of the very soul that once watched over and protected them.
