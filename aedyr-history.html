THE FIRST WAR

A conflict that changed the balance of Aedyr.

22 JULY 1677 — 12 DECEMBER 1683
01 / HISTORICAL OVERVIEW

The Beginning of an Era

The First War was a continental conflict between Pride and the other kingdoms of Aedyr. What began as growing political tension eventually developed into a war that would determine the future of the Seven Kingdoms.

Following Reiko's rise to power and Pride's independence from Greed, the balance of power across the continent began to change. Pride gradually grew stronger, while relations between the kingdoms became increasingly strained.

On July 22, 1677, Reiko officially declared war against the other kingdoms. Greed, Wrath, Lust, Envy, and eventually Gluttony formed a coalition against Pride. Despite their common enemy, disagreements and conflicting interests weakened the alliance from within.

As the years passed, the coalition gradually fell apart. Kingdoms began to fall, alliances shifted, and Pride continued its campaigns across the continent. On December 12, 1683, the First War officially came to an end.

02 / PARTICIPATING NATIONS

The Seven Kingdoms

Stolzelys

Known as Pride. Led by Reiko and the central power in the First War.

Cleptosacra

Known as Greed. Initially opposed Stolzelys before eventually changing sides.

Afgunspiegel

Known as Envy. Aligned with the coalition against Stolzelys.

Ivremiwa

Known as Lust. Joined the coalition before attacking neutral Letisefja.

Coleranza

Known as Wrath. One of the founding members of the coalition.

Gorgemont

Known as Gluttony. Reluctantly joined the coalition before eventually collapsing.

Letisefja

Known as Sloth. Refused the coalition and declared neutrality.
